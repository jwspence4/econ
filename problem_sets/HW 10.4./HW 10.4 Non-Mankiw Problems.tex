\begin{enumerate}

\item Supply for plastic toys is given by $Q_S = 50P$, and demand for plastic toys is given by $Q_D = 200 - 5P$. Manufacturing the plastic for each toy generates \$2 worth of waste per toy. 

	\begin{enumerate}
	
	\item What quantity of toys is sold at the market equilibrium? 
	
	\item What quantity of toys is sold at the efficient equilibrium? 
	
	\item Is there deadweight loss at the market equilibrium? Why or why not? 
	
	\end{enumerate}
	
\item Tyson is a poultry processing company. Raising and slaughtering a large number of chickens creates significant waste that smells bad and pollutes nearby waterways. Suppose it costs Tyson \$50,000 to deal with the waste in a way that doesn’t affect its neighbors, and suppose its neighbors value fresh air and clean water at \$100,000. Explain what happens in each of the following situations. Think about the Coase theorem.

	\begin{enumerate}
	
	\item Tyson has the right to generate smelly, water polluting waste. Negotiation between Tyson and its neighbors is free.
	
	\item Generating smelly, water-polluting waste is illegal. Negotiation is free.
	
	\item Tyson has the right to generate smelly, water polluting waste. It costs each party \$10,000 to negotiate due to attorney fees.
	
	\end{enumerate}

\end{enumerate}
