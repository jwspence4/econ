\begin{enumerate}

\item At Fenway Park, home of the Boston Red Sox, seating is limited to about 38,000. Hence, the number of tickets issued is fixed at that figure. Seeing a golden opportunity to raise revenue, the City of Boston levies a per ticket tax of \$5 to be paid by the ticket buyer. Boston sports fans, a famously civic-minded lot, dutifully send in the \$5 per ticket. Draw a well-labeled graph showing the impact of the tax. On whom does the tax burden fall--the team's owners, the fans, or both? Why?

\item A market is described by the following supply and demand curves:
	\begin{gather*}
	Q_S = 2P \\
	Q_D = 300 - P.
	\end{gather*}
	
	\begin{enumerate}
	
	\item Solve for the equilibrium price and quantity.
	
	\item If the government imposes a price ceiling of \$90, does a shortage or surplus (or neither) develop? What are the price, quantity supplied, quantity demanded, and size of the shortage or surplus?
	
	\item If the government imposes a price floor of \$90, does a shortage or surplus (or neither) develop? What are the price, quantity supplied, quantity demanded, and size of the shortage or surplus?
	
	\item Instead of a price control, the government levies a \$30 tax on producers. Does a shortage or surplus (or neither) develop? What are the price, quantity supplied, quantity demanded, and size of the shortage or surplus? 
	
	\end{enumerate}
	
\item The market for an hour of tutoring is defined by the following supply and demand curves:
	\begin{gather*}
	Q_D = 2,500 - 3P \\
	Q_S = 5P - 100.
	\end{gather*}
	
	If the government implements a price ceiling of \$300, how much does the quantity of tutoring decrease?
	
\item The market for uber rides is defined by the following supply and demand curves:
	\begin{gather*}
	Q_D = 150 - 5P \\
	Q_S = 5P
	\end{gather*}
	
	\begin{enumerate}
	
	\item If the government implements a price floor of \$20, how much does the quantity of uber rides decrease?
	
	\item If instead the government levied a \$2 tax on uber drivers for ever ride sold, how much would the quantity of rides decrease?
	
	\item If instead the government levied a \$2 tax on uber riders for every ride bought, what would the incidence of the tax be? Please answer in percentages.
	
	\end{enumerate}
	
\item The market for a gallon of gas is defined by the following supply and demand curves:
	\begin{gather*}
	Q_D = 600 - 150P \\
	Q_S = 200P - 100
	\end{gather*}
	
	The government implements a tax of \$0.875 per gallon of gas sold on buyers. 
	
	\begin{enumerate}
	
	\item What is the pre-tax equilibrium price and quantity in this market?
	
	\item After the tax, what is the price consumers pay and the price producers receive?
	
	\item What is the after-tax equilibrium quantity in this market? 
	
	\end{enumerate}
	
\end{enumerate}
