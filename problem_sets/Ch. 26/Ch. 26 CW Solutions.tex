Interpretation 1: countries with more stable legal and political environments and countries with stronger protections on property rights create an environment that is more conducive to investing in physical capital and generating technological knowledge. 	\item Higher income gives people more money to invest in both preventative and curative care. It also gives them more money to spend on increasing technological knowledge related to health.
	
	\item Better health outcomes boost human capital. Human capital boosts productivity. Productivity boosts income. 
	
	\item The relative importance determines the degree to which public policy should target health improvements or income improvements. If health is more determinative of income, then public policy should target health more. If income is more determinative of health, then public policy should target income more.

\item Peace and a coherent justice system make it easier to invest in physical and human capital because the capital isn't under threat from war or tenuous property rights. Additionally, peace and a coherent justice system create an environment in which it is easier to advance technological knowledge. If we interpret "easy" taxes as well-designed taxes, then they are a form of technological knowledge. Taxes raise revenue for public goods but generate deadweight loss and reduce production. A well-designed tax system will raise revenue for public goods while minimizing the disincentives on production.
