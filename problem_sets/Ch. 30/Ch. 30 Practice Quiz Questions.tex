\section*{Instructions} Please answer the following on another sheet of paper and justify your answers.

\begin{enumerate}

\item Explain why stocks aren't considered money in the U.S. economy based on the three functions of money.

\item What is the dual mandate of the Federal Reserve?

\item The money supply is \$480 million. The currency held by the public is twice the reserves held by banks. Banks have a reserve ratio of 0.1. What are the levels of currency and deposits in the economy?

\item A bank has \$1,000 in deposits and \$600 of debt. It maintains a reserve ratio of 10\%. It holds a portfolio of securities worth \$800 and has \$1,100 of loans outstanding.

	\begin{enumerate}
	
	\item What is the value of bank capital? Please write out the bank's balance sheet.
	
	\item What is the bank's leverage ratio?
	
	\item The bank's securities increase in value by 25\%. What is the percent change in the bank's assets? What is the percent change in bank capital?
	
	\end{enumerate}
	
\item Explain whether each of the following events increases or decreases the money supply.

	\begin{enumerate}
	
	\item The Fed lowers the interest rate it pays on reserves.
	
	\item The Fed raises the discount rate.
	
	\item The Fed sells U.S. treasuries on the open market. 
	
	\end{enumerate}

\end{enumerate}
