\begin{enumerate}

\item What type of externality (positive or negative) is present in each of the following examples? Is the social value of the activity greater than or equal to the private value? Is the social cost of the activity greater than or equal to the private cost? Without intervention, will there be too little or too much (relative to what would be efficient) of this activity?

	\begin{enumerate}
	
	\item Mr. Chau plants lots of colorful flowers in his front yard.
	
	\item Your next-door neighbor likes to build bonfires in his backyard, and sparks often drift onto your house.
	
	\item Maija, who lives next to an apple orchard, decides to keep bees to produce honey.
	
	\item Justine buys a large SUV that consumes a lot of gasoline.
	
	\end{enumerate}
	
\item Suppose that tanneries pollute nearby bodies of water. The supply and demand for leather goods are given by $Q_S = \frac{1}{2}P$ and $Q_D = 10 - 2P$. If the pollution creates a \$3 per unit externality, please answer the following.

	\begin{enumerate}
	
	\item What are the price and quantity at the market equilibrium?
	
	\item What are the price and quantity at the efficient equilibrium?
	
	\item What is deadweight loss at the market equilibrium? 
	
	\item What is total surplus at the efficient equilibrium?
	
	\end{enumerate}
	
\item Supply for solar cells is given by $Q_S = 4P$, and demand is given by $Q_D = 14 - 2P$. Because they reduce carbon emissions, every solar cell sold generates \$2 worth of external value.

	\begin{enumerate}
	
	\item What quantity of solar cells is sold at the market equilibrium?
	
	\item What  quantity of solar cells is sold at the efficient equilibrium? 
	
	\item What government policy would generate the efficient equilibrium?
	
	\end{enumerate}
	
\item Suppose the government implemented a carbon tax of \$20 per ton of carbon dioxide emissions.

	\begin{enumerate}
	
	\item Draw a supply-and-demand diagram for carbon dioxide emissions. Let the $x$-axis units be tons and the $y$-axis units be dollars per ton. Indicate on your diagram the equilibrium price and quantity of emissions with and without the tax.
	
	\item Demonstrate on your diagram what would happen to the price and quantity of emissions if the government raised the carbon tax.
	
	\item Suppose the government replaced the carbon tax with a cap-and-trade system, but they targeted the same quantity of pollution as they did with the \$20 tax. Draw a new supply-and-demand diagram, and indicate the equilibrium price and quantity of emissions under the cap-and-trade system. Keep the same units as before.
	
	\item If one permit allows for one ton of carbon dioxide emissions, what is the price of a pollution permit under the cap-and-trade system?
	
	\end{enumerate}

\end{enumerate}
