\begin{enumerate}

\item The market for an hour of legal advice is defined by the following supply and demand curves:
	\begin{gather*}
	Q_D = 2,500 - 3P \\
	Q_S = 5P - 100.
	\end{gather*}
	
	If the government implements a price ceiling of \$300, how much does the quantity of legal advice decrease?
	
\item The market for uber rides is defined by the following supply and demand curves:
	\begin{gather*}
	Q_D = 150 - 5P \\
	Q_S = 5P
	\end{gather*}
	
	\begin{enumerate}
	
	\item If the government implements a price floor of \$20, how much does the quantity of uber rides decrease?
	
	\item If instead the government levied a \$2 tax on uber drivers for ever ride sold, how much would the quantity of rides decrease?
	
	\item If instead the government levied a \$2 tax on uber riders for every ride bought, how would the tax burden be distributed between consumers and producers? Please answer in percentages.
	
	\end{enumerate}

\item The market demand and supply equations for high-sugar beverages are given below:
	\begin{gather*}
	Q_D = 100 - 2P \\
	Q_S = -20 + 4P
	\end{gather*}
	The government implements a \$3 per-unit tax on high-sugar beverages. 
	
	\begin{enumerate}
	
	\item How much does the tax decrease the quantity of high-sugar beverages consumed?
	
	\item How much of the \$3 tax is paid by the consumer, and how much is paid by the producer?
	
	\item What does your answer to part (b) tell us about the relative elasticities of supply and demand?
	
	\end{enumerate}

\item Consider the local labor market for entry-level warehouse workers in Trinitopia. Labor demand and labor supply are represented by the following equations:
	\begin{gather*}
	L_D = 50 - 2W \\
	L_S = -10 + 2W
	\end{gather*}
	The city council is considering implementing a minimum wage of \$18 per hour.
	\begin{enumerate}
	
	\item Calculate the equilibrium wage and the equilibrium number of workers employed in the absence of government intervention.
	
	\item Calculate employment if the city council implements its proposed minimum wage.
	
	\item Calculate unemployment if the city council implements its proposed minimum wage.
	
	\end{enumerate}

\end{enumerate}
