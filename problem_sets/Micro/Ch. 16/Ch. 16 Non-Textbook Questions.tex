\item Assume that a monopolist sells a product with a total cost function $TC = 1{,}200 + 0.5Q^2$. The market demand equation is $Q = 300 - P$.
	\begin{enumerate}
	
	\item What is the inverse market demand equation?
	
	\item Please write the monopolist's total revenue as a function of $Q$. There shouldn't be a $P$ in your final answer.
	
	\item What is the monopolist's marginal revenue as a function of $Q$?
	
	\item What is the monopolist's marginal cost?
	
	\item What quantity will the monopolist produce, and what price will they charge for their good?
	
	\end{enumerate}

\end{enumerate}
